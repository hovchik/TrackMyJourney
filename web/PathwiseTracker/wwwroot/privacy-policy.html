Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pathwise Tracker ("the App", package name com.trackjourney) collects, uses, and protects your information. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Location Data

When you start a tracking session, the App collects:

  • GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude)
  • Altitude
  • Speed
  • Bearing (direction)
  • Location accuracy
  • Timestamps

The App uses background location access (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) to continue tracking when the App is minimized or the screen is off. This is used only during active tracking sessions that you explicitly start.

1.2 Health Data

When a compatible Bluetooth LE smartwatch (Garmin or Samsung) is connected, the App collects:

  • Heart rate (BPM)
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2 %)
  • Device name and type

Health data is collected only during active tracking sessions and only from devices you explicitly pair.

1.3 Activity Data

The App uses the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission and on-device AI to detect your movement type (walking, running, cycling, driving, flying, or stationary). This classification is performed entirely on your device.

1.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or account information
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting SDKs
  • We do not track you outside of active recording sessions

2. How We Use Your Information

Data Type Purpose Storage
GPS location Display your route on the map, calculate distance and speed Local device only
Health metrics Display heart rate and SpO2 alongside your route, generate health insights Local device only
Activity type Automatically classify your movement and color-code route segments Local device only
Session metadata Organize and display your tracking history Local device only

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted Room database.
  • Exported JSON files are saved to the App's private storage directory (Android/data/com.trackjourney/files/tracks/).
  • No data is uploaded to cloud servers or external databases by default.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties.

The only scenario in which your data leaves your device is if you voluntarily use the webhook live-tracking feature, which transmits location data in real time to a web server you specify. You control when this feature is active and what server receives the data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may process limited data:

  • OpenStreetMap tile servers: When you view the map, your device downloads map tiles based on the visible area. The tile server may log standard HTTP request data (IP address, requested tile coordinates). This is governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services (Fused Location Provider): Used to obtain GPS coordinates. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Your tracking data is retained on your device until you choose to delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual tracking sessions from within the App
  • Export your data before deletion
  • Uninstall the App to remove all stored data

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data through the App, please contact us so we can assist.

8. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can:

  • Access all your data through the App's track history and export features
  • Export your data as JSON files
  • Delete any or all tracking sessions
  • Revoke permissions at any time via your device's Settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
