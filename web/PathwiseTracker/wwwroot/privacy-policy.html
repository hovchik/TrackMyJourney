Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pathwise Tracker ("the App", package name com.trackjourney) collects, uses, stores, retains, and deletes your information. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring full transparency about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Location Data

Pathwise Tracker accesses and collects precise location data (GPS). When you start a tracking session, the App collects the following location data from your device:

  • GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude) — to record your route and display it on the map
  • Altitude — to display elevation changes along your route
  • Speed — to calculate and display your movement speed
  • Bearing (direction) — to show the direction of travel
  • Location accuracy — to assess the reliability of GPS readings
  • Timestamps — to create a time-ordered record of your journey

How location data is used: Location data is used solely to record your route, calculate trip statistics (distance, speed, elevation), display your journey on a map, and enable on-device AI activity classification. Location data is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers.

The App requires background location access (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) to continue tracking when the App is minimized or the screen is off. This permission is used only during active tracking sessions that you explicitly start by pressing the record button. The App does not access your location at any other time.

1.2 Health Data

When a compatible Bluetooth LE smartwatch (Garmin or Samsung) is connected, the App collects:

  • Heart rate (BPM)
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2 %)
  • Device name and type

Health data is collected only during active tracking sessions and only from devices you explicitly pair. It is stored locally on your device and used to display health metrics alongside your route.

1.3 Activity Data

The App uses the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission and on-device AI to detect your movement type (walking, running, cycling, driving, flying, or stationary). This classification is performed entirely on your device and is used to annotate and color-code your route segments. This data is never transmitted externally.

1.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or account information
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting SDKs
  • We do not access your location outside of active recording sessions

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted Room database.
  • Exported JSON files are saved to the App's private storage directory (Android/data/com.trackjourney/files/tracks/).
  • No data is uploaded to our cloud servers or external databases by default.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties.

The only scenario in which your data leaves your device is if you voluntarily enable the webhook live-tracking feature, which transmits location data in real time to a web server URL that you configure. You have full control over when this feature is active and which server receives the data. We do not operate or have access to that server.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may process limited data:

  • OpenStreetMap tile servers: When you view the map, your device downloads map tiles based on the visible area. The tile server may log standard HTTP request data (IP address, requested tile coordinates). This is governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services (Fused Location Provider): Used to obtain GPS coordinates on Android devices. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Pathwise Tracker does not operate any external servers or databases, so no user data is retained by us. All data collected by the App is stored exclusively on your device.

Retention period: Your tracking data (location, health metrics, activity records) is retained on your device indefinitely until you choose to delete it. The App does not automatically delete data after any fixed period. Data persists until you take one of the deletion actions described below, or until you uninstall the App.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time using any of the following methods:

  • Delete individual sessions: Open the App, go to the Tracks screen, select a session, and choose "Delete". The session and all associated location, health, and activity data for that session will be permanently removed from your device.
  • Delete all data via uninstall: Uninstalling the App removes all data stored in the App's private storage, including the encrypted database and any exported files located in Android/data/com.trackjourney/.
  • Clear App data: On your Android device, go to Settings → Apps → Pathwise Tracker → Storage → Clear Data. This permanently erases all App data including all tracking sessions.

Because all data is stored locally on your device, deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no server-side copy to delete.

To request assistance with data deletion, contact us at support@pathwisetracker.com.

8. Health Data Disclaimer

Pathwise Tracker is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. The App is not approved or cleared by any regulatory authority (such as the FDA or CE) as a medical device.

Health-related data displayed in the App — including heart rate, blood oxygen saturation (SpO2), and calorie estimates — is derived from consumer-grade Bluetooth sensors and on-device calculations. This data is provided for general fitness and informational purposes only and may not be accurate or reliable for medical use.

Do not rely on data from this App to make medical decisions. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data through the App, please contact us so we can assist.

10. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can:

  • Access all your data through the App's track history and export features
  • Export your data as JSON files
  • Delete any or all tracking sessions (see Section 7 above)
  • Revoke location permissions at any time via your device's Settings → Apps → Pathwise Tracker → Permissions
